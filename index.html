        About

        Free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) guide to ancient sites.

        Data sources

        • Pilot sites: hand-checked facts from Wikipedia, Historic England, English Heritage and UNESCO (links on each site).
        • World Heritage Sites: names, coordinates, country, category and year from Wikidata (CC0), keyed by UNESCO WHS ID (P757), linked to the official UNESCO World Heritage List. Wikidata may differ from UNESCO; the UNESCO page is authoritative. UNESCO descriptions are not republished (UNESCO terms: Copyright © 1992–2026 UNESCO/World Heritage Centre. All rights reserved; republication needs written permission).
        • Add more sites via data/extra_sites.json.
        • Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

        Privacy notice (UK GDPR)

        No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads, and we never sell data. Your location is used in your browser for "Near me"; it is sent to the server only when you ask a question or submit a report, and is not stored with questions. Offline packs and queued items stay on your device until sent or deleted. Reports (photos, GPS, time, notes) are stored privately for review by heritage experts under legitimate interest / public-interest archaeology, and may be passed to the relevant authority (e.g. Finds Liaison Officer / Portable Antiquities Scheme, Egypt's Supreme Council of Antiquities). Exact coordinates are never shown publicly; the public map shows only ~1 km counts. Questions, photos you take with "Ask" and your approximate location are sent to this server to find the nearest site, and may be sent to the configured AI provider when live AI is on. You can delete your report at any time with your report code. You can contact the operator to exercise your rights or complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
